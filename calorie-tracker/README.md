# Mochi Bites 🍡

A cute little calorie tracker. Mochi sits in the middle of your progress ring and reacts to your day: sleepy when nothing's logged, hungry, happy, cheering at your goal, and a bit stuffed if you go over.

## Features

- Daily calorie goal (800–6,000 kcal) with a progress ring
- Add food by name and kcal, sorted into breakfast, lunch, dinner or snacks. The emoji is picked from the food's name.
- Quick-add chips for common foods (rough, typical-portion estimates)
- Water tracker (8 cups)
- Week chart with a goal line. Tap a bar to open that day.
- Browse past days with ‹ and ›. Tap the date to jump back to today.
- Light and dark mode; animations turn off when reduced motion is on

Data is saved in the browser (`localStorage`) on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Run it

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
npx serve calorie-tracker
```
