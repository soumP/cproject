# Tummy Pals 🐾

A calorie tracker where you adopt a little pet, and logging your food feeds it.

## How it works

- **Adopt a pal:** choose a kitty, bunny, puppy or bear, give it a name, and set your daily calorie goal. You can change these any time with ⚙️.
- **Feed it:** each food you log flies from the bowl into your pet's mouth, and it munches happily. Type the food and calories, or tap a quick treat (roti, dal, chai, samosa… rough estimates).
- **Moods:** hungry with sparkly eyes when its tummy is empty, happy and playful as you eat, proud ⭐ when you're near your goal, and sleepy when it's very full. It never gets sad or tells you off. Its thought bubble shows what it wants (🍙 food, 💧 water).
- **Water:** tap the 8 cups to fill its water bowl.
- **Pet it:** tap your pet for hearts.
- **Level up:** you earn XP for logging food (+5), drinking water (+3), petting (+1), and finishing a day near your goal (+20). XP comes from your log, so deleting a mistake also takes away its XP.
- **Wardrobe:** levelling up unlocks 🎀 bow, 👓 specs, 🌸 flower crown, 🧣 scarf, 🥳 party hat and 👑 crown.
- **Room:** it changes with the time of day (morning, day, evening, night). At night your pet falls asleep.
- **Diary:** shows your week with 🐾 for days you logged food and ⭐ for days you hit your goal. Food is grouped by meal, and you can open past days to add or remove entries.
- **🔥 Streak** of days in a row with food logged.

Everything is saved in this browser (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent anywhere. Food logs from the earlier Mochi Bites version carry over automatically.

## Run it

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
npx serve calorie-tracker
```
